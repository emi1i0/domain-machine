# Scoring rubric

Score every shortlisted **name** (not domain) 0–10 on each criterion, then compute the weighted
total. Be honest and spread scores out — a rubric where everything gets 7–8 is useless. Show the
breakdown for the top picks so the user can disagree with specific judgements.

| Criterion | Weight | 10 looks like | 3 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Memorability** | 0.20 | Sticks after one hearing; vivid image or sound (`slack`, `stripe`) | Generic or forgettable (`datasolutionspro`) |
| **Radio test** | 0.15 | Hear it once → type it right (`notion`) | Multiple plausible spellings (`fliqr`, `kwik`) |
| **Pronounceability** | 0.10 | One obvious pronunciation across target languages | Speakers will argue how to say it |
| **Relevance / fit** | 0.15 | Evokes the product or its feeling, matches tone | Unrelated or wrong tone (playful name for a bank) |
| **Distinctiveness** | 0.15 | Ownable, nothing similar in the space; trademark-friendly | Crowded word; competitors with similar names |
| **Brevity** | 0.10 | ≤ 6 chars / 2 syllables | > 14 chars / 5+ syllables |
| **Domain quality** | 0.15 | Exact-match `.com` (or the category's best TLD) free at standard price | Only modifier/obscure TLD free, or premium-priced |

`score = Σ weight × criterion`, rounded to one decimal.

## Domain quality sub-guide

- 10: bare `name.com` available at standard price.
- 8–9: bare name on the category's strongest TLD (`.ai` for AI, `.io`/`.dev` for dev tools, `.app` for apps)
  and `.com` taken only by an unrelated, inactive or parked holder.
- 6–7: bare name on a good TLD, `.com` held by an active but unrelated business; or `get`/`use`/`hq`
  modifier on `.com`.
- 4–5: modifier on a secondary TLD, or the `.com` belongs to something adjacent (confusion risk).
- 0–3: only weak TLDs, premium pricing, or a same-space competitor owns the `.com`.

## Hard penalties (apply after weighting, and mention them)

- −2: an active company in the same industry uses the same/similar name.
- −2: registered trademark found in the target classes/markets (see research playbook).
- −1: negative meaning or slang in a target-market language.
- −1: hyphen or digit not essential to the brand.
- −0.5: renewal price > 2× first-year price on the recommended domain.

## Ranking the shortlist

Rank by score, but the final "Top picks" list should also be **diverse**: don't present 10 variants
of the same root. Prefer at most 2 per root, and show at least 3 different strategies when possible.

For each top pick the report needs: best domain, other free TLDs, price (1st year / renewal),
strategy, one-line rationale, risks, trademark verdict, handle availability.
